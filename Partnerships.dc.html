Request availability

Multi-video campaigns and product-only collaborations are considered case by case. Custom scopes are welcome — tell me the outcome you need and I'll tell you what it takes.

What you receive

Every campaign, the same package.

  • A finished video with your product integrated as agreed
  • Clear FTC disclosure in the video, description and pinned comment
  • Tracked link placement in the description
  • Review and revision rounds before publishing
  • A day-7 and day-30 report with Studio screenshots
  • Honest feedback on the product, whether or not you use it

Reporting

Two reports per campaign: day 7 and day 30. Views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention on the sponsored segment, likes, comments and link clicks where you can share them — sent as Studio screenshots so you can see the source, not a re-typed summary.

No view guarantees, ever. Performance is reported, not promised.

Disclosure

Paid work is labelled as paid — YouTube's paid-promotion flag, a spoken disclosure near the start, a line in the description, and a pinned comment. This is non-negotiable and it protects both of us.

Products provided for coverage are disclosed as gifted. Scripts can be reviewed, but final editorial judgement stays with me.

The process
